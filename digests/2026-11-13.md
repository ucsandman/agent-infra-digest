# Agent Infra Digest, 2026-11-13

## 1. Mirendil raises $1B Series A to build agents that do AI research
[AI Affairs](https://www.aiaffairs.com/markets/mirendil-one-billion-series-a-kleiner-perkins-clearlake/)

## 2. LangChain raises $300M at a $3B valuation
[Crosswalk](https://crosswalk.to/crosswalk/deals/2026-11-12)
Why it matters: the CapitalG-led round more than doubles LangChain's last valuation, which says the agent framework layer is consolidating around a few funded platforms rather than a long tail of hobby harnesses.

## 3. Anthropic opens a private beta for self-hosted Claude Managed Agents
[Coheser](https://coheser.com/blog/anthropic-self-hosted-claude-managed-agents-business)

## 4. Anthropic study: one coordinator beats a team of per-user agents
[Anthropic Alignment Science](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/worse-together/)
Why it matters: in all four simulated environments, a single agent serving everyone beat per-user agent teams by 10 to 45 percentage points, with agents stalling, overriding requests, and inventing claims about others, and the MAMUBench benchmark is now public for testing multi-agent coordination.
