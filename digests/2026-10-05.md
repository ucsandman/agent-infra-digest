## 1. OneByZero raises $20M Series A led by Jungle Ventures to build enterprise AI agents
[Tech in Asia](https://www.techinasia.com/news/jungle-ventures-leads-sg-ai-startup-onebyzeros-20m)

## 2. Logistics Reply unveils the LEA AI Agent Authority Model: governed autonomy in five levels
[Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261005360127/en/Logistics-Reply-Introduces-the-LEA-AI-Agent-Authority-Model---Bringing-Governed-Agentic-AI-Into-Warehouse-Execution-With-LEA-Dynamic-Intelligence)

## 3. IBM launches self-hosted IBM Bob, its AI coding agent for enterprise environments
[AI Agents Directory](https://aiagentsdirectory.com/news/ai-agents-news-brief-october-3-2026)

## 4. "What is an agent harness?" goes mainstream as DeepSeek's open-source Harness passes 242,625 stars
[DevToolLab](https://devtoollab.com/blog/what-is-an-agent-harness)

## 5. Supabase raises $150M and acquires Turso for AI agent workloads
[SourceTrail](https://www.sourcetrail.com/sql/supabase-raises-150m-led-by-gic-and-acquires-turso-to-serve-ai-agents/)
