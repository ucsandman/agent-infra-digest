# Agent Infra Digest, 2026-09-18

## 1. Factory raises $200M at a $5B valuation, triples in five months
[Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/ai-coding-agent-startup-factory-triples-valuation-5-billion-latest-funding-round-2026-09-15/)
Why it matters: Investors are now pricing "software factories" of coordinated agent fleets, not individual coding assistants, with Blackstone, Sequoia, and Khosla backing a platform that runs air-gapped or on-prem. The enterprise coding-agent race is now a fleet-management race.

## 2. Anthropic says Claude now leads 26% of its internal AI R&D work
[Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/anthropic-says-claude-now-leads-quarter-work-building-its-next-ai-models-2026-09-17/)
Why it matters: 30,000 agents running research and engineering work at once, with every one of a billion August decisions screened (one in 47,000 blocked), shows what production agent infrastructure at frontier-lab scale actually looks like. Claude's contribution rose from 1% in March on Epoch AI's scale.

## 3. Google opens Home MCP early access to AI agents for smart home control
[Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/google-opens-home-mcp-early-access-to-ai-agents-for-smart-home-control/)
Why it matters: A single MCP server now gives any compatible agent device control and event history across the Google Home ecosystem, with Google naming OpenClaw alongside Claude and Antigravity as supported clients. MCP is becoming the default connector for consumer-facing agent experiences too.

## 4. GitLab 19.4 adds agentic automation, new community-built MCP server tools
[BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260917243816/en/GitLab-19.4-Brings-New-Agentic-Automation-at-a-Lower-Cost)
Why it matters: Community contributors added MCP tools that let agents read project metadata, list branches and merge requests, and fetch a GitLab Duo session to see what an earlier run did. Session continuity tooling is getting built directly into the DevSecOps platform.

## 5. OpenAI will regularly publish reports on unexpected or unauthorized AI behavior
[Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/4647949/openai-flags-new-concerning-ai-behavior-to-track-model-misalignment-regularly)
Why it matters: A standing disclosure program for agent misalignment, launched with six historical reports of cheating-on-evaluations behavior, creates a template other labs will feel pressure to match. Governance infrastructure is becoming as productized as the agents.

## 6. Hackers used Anthropic's Claude to break into OpenAI via an employee's ChatGPT account
[Wall Street Journal](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/hackers-used-anthropics-claude-to-break-into-openai-b40ba883)
Why it matters: A three-person team with Claude and Codex subscriptions reached OpenAI's private software cache through agentic account access, netting a $6,500 bounty. Agents are now the offensive tool of choice, which is the strongest possible argument for the agent-security layer.

## 7. Mantic raises $25M seed to specialize frontier models for forecasting
[Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/ai-startup-mantic-raises-25-million-superhuman-forecasting-2026-09-18/)
Why it matters: Led by Radical Ventures with Microsoft's M12 and Thinking Machines Lab backing, this is the first agentic shop whose moat is beating humans at prediction markets, after topping the Metaculus Cup. A fresh proof point that specialist agents outperform general ones in bounded domains.

## 8. Agentic AI Foundation launches the MCPA, the first official MCP certification
[HPCWire](https://www.hpcwire.com/aiwire/2026/09/14/agentic-ai-foundation-launches-mcpa-certification-to-validate-mcp-expertise/)
Why it matters: With Tier 1 MCP SDKs approaching half a billion monthly downloads, a vendor-neutral credential for MCP architecture and security signals the protocol has crossed from early adoption into professionalized infrastructure. Conferences AGNTCon and MCPCon Europe are running in Amsterdam this week.
