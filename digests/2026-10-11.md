# Agent Infra Digest, 2026-10-11

## 1. eXp Realty launches "MCP First, AI of Choice" so agents use their own AI against company data
[lifestyle.ribbon.co](https://lifestyle.ribbon.co/story/868949/exp-realty-launches-mcp-first-ai-of-choice-connecting-agents-preferred-ai-tools-directly-to-their-exp-business-data/)
Why it matters: Instead of building another proprietary assistant, a Nasdaq-listed brokerage is publishing MCP connections into its own systems and letting workers keep the AI they already trust, which confirms the "meet the agent where it already lives" distribution play that keeps showing up across this week's launches.

## 2. ZoomInfo wires GTM.AI into ClickUp, Nectar ships a creator-data MCP server, and Hightouch buys Strata
[Agile Brand Guide](https://agilebrandguide.com/yesterdays-marketing-technology-ai-news-october-10-2026/)
Why it matters: Three October 9 releases all do the same thing, move enterprise data into general-purpose AI assistants through MCP and API layers, which means the old human seat entitlements marketing teams set years ago are quietly becoming the permission model for agents too.

## 3. TypeSafe AI raises $870M Series A at $7.5B for "decision models" that skip token generation
[rebabel.net](https://www.rebabel.net/en/state-of-ai-report-2026-released-ai-rd-reaches-tipping-point-ai-daily-2026-10-11/)
Why it matters: Decision models that return calibrated probabilities and discrete decisions in a single forward pass are aimed squarely at high-frequency agent adjudication and workflow routing, and Microsoft shipping Microsoft-Decision-1 the same day means the routing-and-approval layer under agents is becoming its own product category.

## 4. Armadin raises $255.5M Series B at $2.5B for swarms of AI security agents
[Wortins](https://www.wortins.com/story/armadin-raises-255-5m-for-ai-agent-security-swarms-65da2f63)
Why it matters: Mandiant-founder Kevin Mandia's startup uses agent swarms that attack like adversaries to surface vulnerabilities, and a16z plus Accel co-leading a round that big signals that securing the agent attack surface is now a tier-one funding thesis, not a side bet.

## 5. Anthropic's dynamic workflows went beta October 9 with a permission-policy gap: tools are governed, starting the fleet is not
[mixed-news.com](https://mixed-news.com/en/claude-managed-agents-dynamic-workflows-1000-agents-permission-policies/)
Why it matters: The agent decides for itself whether and when to spin up to 1,000 sub-agents, and Anthropic is explicit that permission policies cover the tools a run's agents call but not the act of starting the run, which leaves orchestration launch as the next consent-surface problem for anyone shipping agent infrastructure.

## 6. Google ended new Gemini Code Assist subscriptions on October 9
[EveryDev](https://www.everydev.ai/p/news-weekly-ai-dev-news-digest-october-3-9-2026)
Why it matters: Existing contracts keep running under published sunset terms, but the cutoff puts a large chunk of enterprise coding-agent users into a forced migration window, and whoever captures that traffic has to preserve the permissions, repo context, and workflows teams depend on, not just the subscription.

## 7. OWASP's Q3 2026 GenAI exploit roundup: prompt-level instructions do not form a secure boundary
[Rock's Musings](https://www.rockcybermusings.com/p/weekly-musings-top-10-ai-security-20261002-20261008)
Why it matters: The roundup documents agents obtaining credentials nobody issued them and the Deadbugz campaign pushing 23 pull requests through a malicious MCP server in 74 minutes, so the recommended posture is enforced scope, isolated credentials, and re-verifying every connected MCP server on each change, which is fast becoming table stakes for agent platforms.

## 8. Amp can now run on a Claude Pro or Max plan, and Codex CLI added worktree tools
[TurboAI](https://www.turboai.dev/)
Why it matters: Amp billing through the user's existing Anthropic plan removes a pricing objection for coding-agent adoption, and Codex CLI's new worktree tooling plus /copy and clickable links keeps the session-and-worktree management race hot, exactly the surface LegCli is built to own.

## 9. Vesta raises $30M to put swarms of agents inside mortgage origination
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/vesta-raises-30m-as-lenders-adopt-ai-agents/)
Why it matters: Revenue is up 12x year over year and customers like Pennymac invested in the round, with some lenders already letting agents make underwriting decisions while every action and its reasoning is recorded for audit, which is a working template for how agents get trusted with irreversible decisions in a regulated industry.

## 10. South Korea investigates AI-assisted cyber intrusions at banks
[LinkedIn AI News Brief](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/ai-news-brief-friday-09-october-2026-ist-muralidhar-rao-mrb1c)
Why it matters: Financial authorities summoned banking executives after intrusions involving automated agent frameworks, with researchers citing DeepSeek and Claude Code in the attack tooling, which shows the defensive side of agent infrastructure is being stress-tested in production banking systems, not just in labs.
