## 2. Cursor traces its October breach to a Fireworks AI key, closing the loop on the backdoored Claude Code extension
[Cursor](https://cursor.com/blog/security-incident-update#what-happened)
Why it matters: the November 13 update says an access key exposed by Fireworks let an attacker copy coding-agent traces and plant malicious Anthropic.claude-code 2.1.312 and 2.1.313 builds that reached at most 5,000 users, and Cursor has now moved all inference off Fireworks, which makes vendor key hygiene a first-class risk for every coding agent.
