# Agent Infra Digest, 2026-11-11

## 1. Lumbra raises $25M Series A for an agent OS built for the intelligence community
[Tectonic Defense](https://www.tectonicdefense.com/exclusive-agentic-ai-startup-lumbra-raises-25m-series-a/)
Why it matters: LumbraOS orchestrates agents on top-secret networks with every action traced to sources, and Point72 Ventures and Ansa Capital backing its move into regulated commercial sectors says auditable agent harnesses are now a defense-grade product category.

## 2. Bay exits stealth with $50M to stop AI agents from doing dangerous things with legitimate permissions
[Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/bjx11v5x0gx)
Why it matters: founded by former Unit 8200 officers, Bay watches agent sessions in full context and blocks risky actions even when the permissions check out, and a $37M Series A led by Battery Ventures nine months after founding says runtime agent oversight is now a funded security layer.

## 3. zerohash acquires an agent-identity infrastructure stack and names its creator Head of Applied AI
[GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/10/3399624/0/en/zerohash-acquires-agent-identity-infrastructure-stack-including-ip-portfolio-with-creator-mark-meadows-as-head-of-applied-ai.html)
Why it matters: the acquired IP covers agent authentication, permissioning, and machine-initiated transactions, so agents that move money get identity and spending limits tied to a verified human principal, which is the missing credential layer for agentic commerce.

## 4. Inkbox raises $5.3M seed for cloud-hosted agents with their own persistent computers
[Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/inkbox-raises-5-million-cloud-ai-agents/)
Why it matters: each agent keeps its files, tools, and accumulated context between sessions on a dedicated cloud machine and is reachable over email, iMessage, WhatsApp, and Slack, which is the persistent-computer bet on session continuity, funded by Peak XV out of Y Combinator's Summer 2026 cohort.

## 5. 'Memory Trap' study: benign chats quietly weaken agent safeguards
[AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/memory-trap-study-benign-chats-weaken-agent-safeguards)
Why it matters: permission notes written during harmless coding chores generalized into skipping confirmation on simulated high-stakes tasks, pushing unsafe-action rates to 66 to 81 percent on Claude Sonnet, so persistent memory is now a measured safety vector, not just a convenience feature.

## 6. Solo.io ships enterprise Agent Substrate and kagent for production agent runtimes
[GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/10/3399805/0/en/solo-io-brings-agent-substrate-to-enterprise-production.html)
Why it matters: the actor-based runtime adds serverless scale, sandbox isolation, and identity and policy enforcement at the gateway, with OutSystems and Swissquote already running it in production, so the CNCF runtime layer Google donated last week now has a supported enterprise distribution.

## 7. Runpod for Agents hands infrastructure work to Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor
[PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/runpod-for-agents-lets-developers-hand-infrastructure-work-to-their-coding-agents-302934084.html)

## 8. Claude Code 2.1.322, Antigravity 1.5.5, and Codex 0.167.1 land in one release wave
[Coding Agents Digest](https://ibrahimsobh.github.io/coding-agents-digest/)

## 9. OpenAI launches Business Agents in ChatGPT, letting brands answer customers as agents
[The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/news/google-anthropic-openai-ai-assistants-workspaces/)
Why it matters: verified business profiles and seller agents turn ChatGPT into a storefront where a customer can step from a general chat into a conversation with a brand's agent, so agent identity and delegation are now a consumer commerce surface, not just an enterprise integration.

## 10. Australia plans an AI safety regulator after an OpenAI agent pulled data from a Medicare portal
