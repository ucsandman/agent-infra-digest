# Agent Infra Digest, 2026-10-12

## 1. The UK ICO pulls AI agents into active supervision and opens a call for evidence
[aitoolbible.com](https://aitoolbible.com/post.php?slug=the-ico-pulls-ai-agents-into-active-supervision-and-questions-openai-meta-and-anthropic)
Why it matters: The regulator opened an agentic-AI call for evidence on October 8, closing November 20, while securing voluntary changes from Amazon, Anthropic, Apple, Cohere, DeepSeek, Google, Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI, and Stability AI, and a statutory code of practice on automated decision-making is now on a drafting path, so agent builders have a short window to shape the baseline rules before they harden.

## 2. Manus closes $500M+ funding round after the Meta acquisition unwound
[thesaasnews.com](https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/manus-raises-500m-other/)
Why it matters: Boyu Capital and IDG Capital co-led the round with Tencent, HSG, and ZhenFund returning, Bloomberg had pegged a $4B valuation, and the raise signals that investors will back an independent autonomous-agent lab even after Chinese regulators forced Meta to reverse its roughly $2B acquisition.

## 3. Anthropic ships Haiku 5.5 at roughly 75% lower cost and halves Sonnet 5.5 cached-context pricing
[sharedsapience.com](https://sharedsapience.com/century-report/the-century-report-october-10-2026/)
Why it matters: A cheaper small model (72.4% on an offline computer-use benchmark, up from 15.7%) plus about 20% lower cost on most agentic work from cache repricing changes the unit economics of agent fleets, making heavy sub-agent orchestration affordable to smaller teams.

## 4. Microsoft and Cloudflare join the decision-model category days after TypeSafe's $870M raise
[sharedsapience.com](https://sharedsapience.com/century-report/the-century-report-october-10-2026/)
Why it matters: Microsoft's Decision-1 (calibrated probabilities, no prose, $0.042 per million input tokens) and Cloudflare's Clef-omni arrived the same week, confirming that routing-and-adjudication models are becoming their own product layer, exactly the scoring surface agent orchestration and approval flows sit on.

## 5. Google launches a universal Gemini agent in private preview for Workspace
[aiagentsdirectory.com](https://aiagentsdirectory.com/news/ai-agents-news-brief-october-10-2026)
Why it matters: One agent spanning chat, code execution, and docs with sub-agent coordination, coworker identities, and spending controls puts Google head to head with Anthropic's Cowork and OpenAI's agents, and consolidation at the platform layer raises the bar for third-party session and handoff tooling.

## 6. Proximal exits stealth with $15M seed at a $300M valuation for post-training infrastructure
[thesaasnews.com](https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/proximal-raises-15m-seed/)
Why it matters: General Catalyst led the round for a platform that turns unstructured real-world data and agent execution traces into evaluation environments, meaning the data and feedback layer under agent reliability is attracting frontier-scale valuations at seed stage.

## 7. Meticulous raises $15M Series A as AI-generated code accelerates testing demand
[aiagentsdirectory.com](https://aiagentsdirectory.com/news/ai-agents-news-brief-october-10-2026)
Why it matters: Funding continues to flow to the verification side of the coding-agent loop, so the quality gate is moving into dedicated infrastructure instead of living on the reviewer's desk.

## 8. Trusty MPM v1.8.0 ships memory hygiene controls and an append-only worktree ledger
[undercodenews.com](https://undercodenews.com/trusty-tools-just-updated-major-security-hardening-and-smarter-ai-memory-arrive-in-october-releases-video/)
Why it matters: Forget-by-key, expiration metadata, a secrets CLI, session rebinding to live tmux panes, and an append-only worktree ledger are precisely the session, memory, and worktree primitives in LegCli territory, so this release is a direct look at how adjacent tooling is answering the same problems.

## 9. A Bit Cloud demo gives a fresh Claude Code session versioned dependency context via MCP
[neoteo.com](https://www.neoteo.com/en/how-mcp-helped-a-fresh-claude-code-session-map-dependencies)
Why it matters: Two MCP calls exposed four versioned components and their dependencies to a brand-new session, which then passed 41 tests, a concrete example of MCP as session-continuity infrastructure rather than just a tool connector.

## 10. A practitioner ships a five-layer memory architecture bridging Antigravity and Obsidian
[blog.voina.org](https://blog.voina.org/giving-local-ai-assistants-long-term-memory-automated-session-archiving-and-context-bridging-with-antigravity-agy-sqlite-and-obsidian/)
